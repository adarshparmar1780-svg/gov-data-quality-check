# Sample data source

`sample_data/all_india_rainfall_1901_1906.csv` contains the first six rows of the India Meteorological Department's All India area-weighted monthly, seasonal, and annual rainfall series. The OGD catalog describes the series as rainfall in millimetres from 1901 to 2015, attributed to the Ministry of Earth Sciences and IMD, and lists the Government Open Data License - India for published datasets.

- [OGD catalog entry](https://www.data.gov.in/catalog/rainfall-india) (see “All India area weighted monthly, seasonal and annual rainfall (in mm) from 1901-2015”)
- [A published preview showing the six sample rows and citing the OGD rainfall source](https://rstudio-pubs-static.s3.amazonaws.com/489019_6399c0c59a3244a58005ffe8332b631d.html)
- [Government Open Data License - India](https://ap.data.gov.in/godl)

`sample_data/demo_with_issues.csv` is synthetic and intentionally contains a repeated row, a blank cell, and a row with an extra field. It is only for demonstrating the checker's output; it is not government data.

This small historical excerpt is a parser example, not a current rainfall report or a statement about data quality across the full source dataset.
